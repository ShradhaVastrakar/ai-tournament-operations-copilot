# Aethon Cup 2025 — Tournament Rules

> **FICTIONAL DATA ONLY.** These rules were created for a portfolio/demo project.
> They are not affiliated with or representative of any real sports federation,
> governing body, or tournament organiser.

---

## 1. Tournament Format

1.1 The Aethon Cup 2025 is a round-robin group stage followed by knockout semi-finals and a final.

1.2 All four participating teams play each other once during the group stage, for a total of six group-stage matches.

1.3 The top two teams by points advance to the semi-finals.

---

## 2. Scoring

2.1 A win awards **3 points**.

2.2 A draw awards **1 point** to each team.

2.3 A loss awards **0 points**.

2.4 Teams level on points are separated first by goal difference, then by goals scored, then by head-to-head result.

---

## 3. Match Rules

3.1 Each match consists of two halves of 45 minutes each, with added time at the referee's discretion.

3.2 Extra time (two 15-minute periods) applies only in knockout-stage matches where the score is level after 90 minutes.

3.3 If the score remains level after extra time, a penalty shoot-out (five kicks per team, then sudden death) determines the winner.

---

## 4. Player Eligibility

4.1 Each squad may register a maximum of 25 players before the tournament starts.

4.2 No mid-tournament registrations are permitted except to replace a player who sustains a season-ending injury, subject to the Tournament Committee's approval.

4.3 A player serving a suspension from a previous sanctioned competition must complete that suspension before becoming eligible.

---

## 5. Disciplinary Rules

5.1 A player who receives **two yellow cards** in the same match is shown a red card and dismissed immediately.

5.2 A straight red card results in a **one-match automatic suspension**.

5.3 Accumulation of **three yellow cards** across separate matches results in a one-match suspension. The counter resets after the suspension is served.

5.4 Serious misconduct may result in additional suspensions at the discretion of the Disciplinary Panel.

---

## 6. Venue and Scheduling

6.1 Match schedules are published at least 14 days in advance.

6.2 Postponements require agreement from both clubs and the Tournament Committee, and must be requested at least 48 hours before kick-off except in cases of force majeure.

6.3 A postponed match must be rescheduled within seven days of the original fixture date.

---

## 7. Result Reporting

7.1 The home team's operations coordinator must submit the official result form within two hours of the final whistle.

7.2 Results with status `pending_result` indicate the match has been played but the official result form has not yet been confirmed by the Tournament Committee.

7.3 Once confirmed, the status changes to `completed` and the record is considered final.

---

## 8. Protests and Appeals

8.1 Any protest against a result must be submitted in writing to the Tournament Committee within 24 hours of the match.

8.2 A non-refundable protest fee of 500 Aethon Credits applies.

8.3 The Committee's decision is final for group-stage matches. Knockout-stage decisions may be appealed to the Aethon Sports Arbitration Board within 48 hours.

---

*End of fictional tournament rules document.*
